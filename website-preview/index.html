Fast answers

Questions North Texas homeowners ask first

Does a cavity mean my tree has to be removed?

No. A cavity is one part of the assessment. Its location, remaining sound wood, tree species, movement, decay indicators, and nearby people or property all matter.

Can I text photos before scheduling?

Yes. Send the whole tree, the concern, and the trunk base with the surrounding ground. Those three views give Arborwise a much better first look.

When is a leaning tree urgent?

A new or increasing lean, lifting soil, cracked ground, broken roots, or movement after a storm deserves prompt attention. Keep people away from the potential fall zone while you call.

Do brown leaves always mean disease?

No. Heat, drought, watering patterns, root damage, soil conditions, insects, disease, and seasonal changes can create similar visible symptoms. The full tree and site need to be considered.
