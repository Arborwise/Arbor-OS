Plan the work before the equipment arrives

Access is part of the tree-care decision.

Arborwise considers where the crew, ropes, saws, equipment, brush, and wood can move without creating a second problem on the property.

  • Identify homes, fences, roads, vehicles, livestock areas, and other targets.
  • Protect lawns, landscaping, gates, drainage, and surfaces where possible.
  • Choose climbing, rigging, lowering, or equipment based on the site.
  • Explain what will be removed and why.
  • Finish with the agreed cleanup and material handling.

Annie’s tip: A large open yard is not permission to drop everything. Roots, fences, septic areas, landscaping, and hidden utilities still matter.

The Arborwise Way

Safety is planning, not a slogan.

The crew should know the objective, the targets, the drop or rigging plan, the escape routes, the cleanup scope, and what changes if conditions are different than expected.

Assess first

Structure, defects, roots, lean, weather, access, targets, and recent changes.

Control the work

Correct cuts, ropes, rigging, lowering, communication, and property protection appropriate to the job.

Explain the result

What was completed, what remains, and what the customer should watch or maintain next.

Fast answers

Howe tree questions

Does Arborwise work on acreage and rural properties?

Yes. Arborwise works on residential, commercial, acreage, HOA, and property-management sites. Access, targets, cleanup, and material handling are discussed before the work.

Can Arborwise remove a tree near a fence or building?

Yes. The crew plans the removal around the site and may use climbing, rigging, controlled lowering, or equipment as appropriate.

Does Arborwise respond to storm-damaged trees?

Yes. Arborwise provides priority storm-damage response and helps separate immediate hazards from damage that can be scheduled methodically.

Can I send photos first?

Yes. Send the whole tree, the concern, and the trunk base with the surrounding property visible.
