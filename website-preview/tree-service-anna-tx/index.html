Pruning in Anna

Good pruning is not simply making the tree smaller.

Bad cuts can weaken structure, create decay points, trigger weak growth, and make future storm damage worse. Arborwise focuses on the objective first.

  • Remove dead, broken, crossing, or rubbing branches.
  • Improve roof, driveway, roadway, and pedestrian clearance.
  • Develop stronger structure in young and maturing trees.
  • Use selective reduction where appropriate instead of topping.
  • Protect the branch collar and make clean cuts in the correct location.

Annie’s tip: A properly pruned tree should still look like its species. The goal is stronger structure, not an empty canopy.
