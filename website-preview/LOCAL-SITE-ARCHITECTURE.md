# Arborwise Local Site Architecture

## Purpose

Build a strong main Arborwise website, then create local entry points that answer city-specific tree questions and move naturally back to the main Arborwise authority site.

## Main site

Primary authority and conversion site:

- arborwisetreecare.com
- Arborwise brand, team, services, reviews, concern checker, educational resources, property-management experience, stories, and contact actions
- All local pages and any future campaign domains support this site

## First-wave local pages

Built on the `website-fresh-start` branch:

- /farmersville-tree-service/
- /tree-service-anna-tx/
- /van-alstyne-tree-service/
- /celina-tree-service/
- /howe-tree-service/
- /princeton-tree-service/
- /service-areas/

The main website links to all six pages. Every local page links back to the main Arborwise site and its concern checker, services, Arborwise Way, and contact options.

## Distinct page focus

- Farmersville: hometown identity, Farmersville Chamber membership, local accountability, community-service history, condition questions, pruning, removal, and storm response
- Anna: established trees, new landscapes, property clearance, young-tree structure, and storm-exposed canopies
- Van Alstyne: mature shade, property targets, controlled work, and Chamber accountability
- Celina: planting depth, trunk flare, root zones, construction effects, young-tree structure, and mature-tree care
- Howe: whole-property planning, acreage, outbuildings, access, mature trees, storm damage, and controlled removals
- Princeton: soil, drainage, compaction, construction, root disturbance, canopy symptoms, and matching the solution to the cause

Historical community projects must not be written as current or recurring programs unless Greg confirms they are still active.

## Quality requirements

Each local page must keep improving with real Arborwise evidence rather than city-name substitutions:

- Arborwise jobs and photographs from that city or nearby service area
- Common species and visible problems
- Soil, drainage, wind, drought, storm, development, and construction concerns relevant to the area
- Local pruning, removal, risk, and storm-response examples
- A city-specific review, project story, or property-management example when available
- Clear service-area statement and realistic response expectations
- City-specific title, description, headings, internal links, FAQs, and structured data
- Direct call, text-photo, email, and estimate actions

## Independent domains

Optional city domains can be purchased for brand protection or advertising, but they should normally redirect to the matching Arborwise location page.

Do not publish thin duplicate mini-sites that merely swap city names and point to the same main page. A true standalone local site must be a distinct local resource with substantial original content, useful navigation, and a clear Arborwise relationship.

## Google Business Profile

Do not create separate city profiles unless Arborwise has a legitimate staffed location that independently qualifies. Service-area coverage belongs under the legitimate Arborwise profile and website location pages.
