Tree condition questions

One symptom can have several possible causes.

Brown foliage, dead tips, sparse growth, bark changes, mushrooms, and leaning can involve water, roots, soil, insects, disease, structure, weather, construction, or more than one condition at the same time.

  • We look at the whole canopy, not one leaf.
  • We inspect the trunk base, roots, soil, targets, and recent changes.
  • We separate what is urgent from what can be monitored.
  • We recommend pruning, treatment, removal, or no work based on what the property actually needs.

Farmersville tree questions

Does Arborwise provide tree trimming in Farmersville?

Yes. Arborwise handles deadwood, structural pruning, selective reduction, clearance, canopy balance, and corrective pruning when appropriate.

Can I text photos before scheduling?

Yes. Send one photo of the whole tree, one of the concern, and one of the trunk base and surrounding ground. Those views help us give a better first answer.

Does a cavity mean the tree has to come down?

No. Location, remaining sound wood, species, movement, decay indicators, and what could be struck all matter. A cavity is one piece of the assessment.

Does Arborwise handle storm damage?

Arborwise provides priority storm-damage response. We help separate immediate hazards from damage that can be handled methodically.
