Tree condition questions

One symptom can have several possible causes.

Brown foliage, dead tips, sparse growth, bark changes, mushrooms, and leaning can involve water, roots, soil, insects, disease, structure, weather, construction, or several conditions together.

  • Look at the whole canopy, not one leaf.
  • Inspect the trunk base, roots, soil, targets, and recent changes.
  • Separate what is urgent from what can be monitored.
  • Recommend pruning, treatment, removal, or no work based on what the property actually needs.

Annie’s tip: Three useful photos beat twelve mystery close-ups: the whole tree, the concern, and the trunk base with surrounding ground.
