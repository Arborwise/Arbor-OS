# Arborwise Farmersville Hometown, Van Alstyne Stronghold, and Growth-Market Campaign

## Non-negotiable scope

- Do not change the main Arborwise website as part of this campaign unless Greg explicitly approves a specific main-site change.
- Keep Farmersville visibly in the top tier as Arborwise's hometown market.
- Keep Van Alstyne visibly in the top tier as an established Arborwise stronghold.
- Focus aggressive new-market lead growth on Anna, Melissa, and Celina.
- Track every Arborwise lead, regardless of town or source.

## Market roles

### Farmersville: hometown market

Greg lives in Farmersville and Arborwise is a Farmersville Chamber of Commerce member. Farmersville must be presented as Arborwise's home community, not as another city name added to a service-area list.

Farmersville priorities:

- Lead with Greg's residence and Arborwise's real hometown accountability.
- Feature Farmersville Chamber membership prominently and accurately.
- Use current Arborwise work, local reviews, real photographs, community relationships, and project stories.
- Keep historical community work clearly historical unless Greg confirms it is still active.
- Track Farmersville leads separately while keeping them inside the complete Arborwise lead tracker.
- Maintain strong call, text, email, and estimate paths from the Farmersville page.

### Van Alstyne: protect and expand the stronghold

Arborwise is already popular in Van Alstyne and is a Van Alstyne Chamber of Commerce member. The company must never be presented there as an outsider or an afterthought.

Van Alstyne priorities:

- Keep Arborwise highly visible and locally accountable.
- Feature Van Alstyne Chamber membership prominently and accurately.
- Reinforce the trust and recognition Arborwise has already earned in the community.
- Track Van Alstyne leads separately so the existing stronghold can be protected and expanded.
- Use real Van Alstyne jobs, reviews, photographs, neighborhood knowledge, and project stories.
- Maintain strong call, text, email, and estimate paths from the Van Alstyne page.

### Anna, Melissa, and Celina: aggressive growth markets

These three towns receive the heaviest new-lead acquisition work through dedicated city pages, local proof, useful tree information, search visibility, campaign distribution, and measurable conversion paths.

## Lead goals

Campaign start: July 29, 2026.

By Friday, August 7, 2026, the target is at least:

- 1 qualified Anna lead
- 1 qualified Melissa lead
- 1 qualified Celina lead

Ongoing monthly target:

- 5 qualified Anna leads
- 5 qualified Melissa leads
- 5 qualified Celina leads

Farmersville and Van Alstyne are measured separately as established markets. Do not invent quotas until enough tracked baseline data exists to set meaningful targets.

A qualified lead is a real homeowner, business, HOA, property manager, or property owner with an actual tree-care need who calls, texts, emails, or requests an estimate.

## Lead tracking rule

For every Arborwise lead, record:

- Name and contact information
- Town
- Date and time
- Service needed
- Source and campaign
- Qualified or unqualified
- Estimate scheduled or not
- Won, lost, pending, or no response
- Estimated value and final job value when known

Farmersville, Van Alstyne, Anna, Melissa, and Celina must all be measured separately while remaining inside the complete Arborwise lead tracker.

## Side-site attribution

The priority local pages use the shared `growth-campaign.js` attribution layer wherever it is enabled.

- Every text link adds a visible city source label such as `FARMERSVILLE WEBSITE LEAD`, `VAN-ALSTYNE WEBSITE LEAD`, `ANNA WEBSITE LEAD`, `MELISSA WEBSITE LEAD`, or `CELINA WEBSITE LEAD`.
- Every email subject adds the same city source label.
- Call, text, email, and form clicks push city, channel, source, page, and timestamp data to `window.dataLayer` for analytics.
- The fast-estimate form creates a labeled text containing the customer name, property location, service need, and concern.
- Links from the local pages to other Arborwise pages receive city-specific UTM parameters.

Full phone-call attribution requires city-specific call-tracking numbers or a connected call-tracking provider. Until that is connected, the pages record call-click events while text, email, and form inquiries arrive city-labeled.

## Town-specific strategy

### Farmersville

Lead with Arborwise's hometown position: Greg lives in Farmersville, Arborwise belongs to the Farmersville Chamber, and local reputation follows every recommendation, cut, cleanup, and customer conversation. Use the beautiful Town Square canopies as historical proof of Arborwise pruning work without implying the Christmas-light work is still current.

### Van Alstyne

Lead with established trust, frequent Arborwise presence, Chamber membership, mature shade trees, property targets, controlled climbing and rigging, storm damage, honest recommendations, and clean completion. Protect the town from competitor encroachment while continuing to grow Arborwise's share.

### Anna

Use Greg's authentic local connection: he is from Anna and attended Anna High School. Present this as real community history, not as a gimmick. Combine that connection with correct pruning, storm-exposed canopies, property clearance, young-tree structure, and honest tree-condition reviews.

### Melissa

Build authority through rapid-growth tree problems: planting depth, buried trunk flare, compacted soil, drainage changes, construction disturbance, young-tree structure, tight-lot clearance, storm exposure, and HOA or property-management needs. Do not invent a hometown story.

### Celina

Focus on fast growth, new construction, planting depth, trunk flare, root-zone damage, construction disturbance, young-tree structure, mature-tree preservation, storm exposure, and property clearance.

## Accountability

- Traffic and rankings are supporting signals, not the goal.
- Calls, texts, emails, estimate requests, qualified leads, scheduled estimates, wins, and revenue are the business measures.
- If Anna, Melissa, or Celina produces zero qualified leads during the first 30 days, change the strategy for that town rather than continuing the same approach.
- If Farmersville or Van Alstyne lead volume or conversion declines from its measured baseline, respond quickly with stronger proof, distribution, offers, follow-up, or local visibility.
- Strategy changes may include page angle, offer, paid search, local social distribution, reviews, project proof, calls to action, call tracking, outreach, or traffic source.
